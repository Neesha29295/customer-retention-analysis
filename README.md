# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
**Tools:** Python · Pandas · Seaborn · Matplotlib

## Business Problem
Identify at-risk customer segments and quantify the revenue 
impact of improving retention for an e-commerce platform.

## Dataset
Olist Brazilian E-Commerce — 99,000+ delivered orders  
Source: [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

## Key Findings
- 🔴 47% of customers are At Risk or Lost
- 🔴 Month-1 retention is only 5.5% — 94.5% never return after first order  
- 🟡 Only 19% of customers are Champions or Loyal
- 🟢 A 10% retention improvement = R$ 1.43M incremental revenue

## Recommendations
1. Re-engagement campaign for 22,229 At Risk customers
2. Post-purchase follow-up offer within 48hrs of first delivery
3. Loyalty nudge for 7,373 Potential Loyalists
4. VIP tier to protect 14,961 Champions
